# LLM Pedagogical Evaluation Rubric (Social Studies)

## Purpose

This rubric is used to evaluate the **pedagogical quality, historical accuracy, and instructional responsibility** of LLM-generated social studies content across grade levels.

Scores reflect quality **relative to the intended course level**, including AP-level expectations for analytical depth, evidence use, and historical reasoning where appropriate.

The rubric is applied consistently across:
- Grade 7 Eastern Hemisphere (Ancient World History)
- Grade 9 Global Studies
- AP US History

---

## Scoring Scale

**5 – Excellent | 4 – Strong | 3 – Adequate | 2 – Weak | 1 – Poor**

---

## 1. Factual Accuracy

**Definition:** Information is historically correct and free of errors or misleading claims.

- **5:** Fully accurate; no factual errors or misleading claims  
- **4:** Minor imprecision but no meaningful errors  
- **3:** Generally accurate with small factual gaps  
- **2:** Contains clear factual errors or misleading statements  
- **1:** Multiple or serious factual inaccuracies  

---

## 2. Historical Reasoning

**Definition:** Demonstrates appropriate use of cause and effect, context, perspective, or change over time.

- **5:** Clear, well-developed historical reasoning appropriate to the course level  
- **4:** Reasoning present but uneven or underdeveloped  
- **3:** Basic reasoning with limited analytical depth  
- **2:** Mostly descriptive; little historical reasoning  
- **1:** No historical reasoning present  

---

## 3. Grade-Level Appropriateness

**Definition:** Vocabulary, abstraction, depth, and assumptions align with the intended grade or course.

- **5:** Perfectly calibrated to the intended course level  
- **4:** Slightly over- or under-scaffolded  
- **3:** Generally appropriate but inconsistent  
- **2:** Frequently mismatched to student level  
- **1:** Inappropriate for the intended grade  

---

## 4. Instructional Usefulness

**Definition:** Supports learning and disciplinary thinking, not just content delivery.

- **5:** Actively supports learning (e.g., scaffolds analysis, models reasoning, guides evidence use)  
- **4:** Helpful but could scaffold reasoning more effectively  
- **3:** Somewhat helpful but surface-level  
- **2:** Minimally helpful for learning  
- **1:** Unhelpful or confusing  

---

## 5. Bias & Framing

**Definition:** Avoids distortion, oversimplification, or problematic framing.

- **5:** Nuanced, balanced, and historically responsible  
- **4:** Mostly balanced with minor issues  
- **3:** Some oversimplification without major distortion  
- **2:** Noticeable bias or misleading framing  
- **1:** Problematic or harmful framing  

---

## Binary Flags (Yes / No)

These are recorded separately from rubric scores.

- **Hallucination or factual error present?**  
- **Oversimplification that distorts historical meaning?**

---

## Scoring Notes

- Each generated response receives **one score per rubric category**.
- Short qualitative notes should be added to justify scores and capture failure modes.
- Scores are intended for **comparative analysis across prompt strategies and grade levels**, not for high-stakes student evaluation.
